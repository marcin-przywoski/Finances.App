# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small salon operators and staff who need a private, local-first way to record services, product sales, expenses, invoices, clients, reminders, and backups during everyday business work. They use the app as an operational tool, often moving quickly between records, lists, totals, and follow-up tasks.

## Product Purpose

Finances.App helps a small salon understand and control its finances without an account, backend, tracking, or shared database. Success means the user can confidently enter records, find prior work, see the state of the business, export a backup, and trust that private data stays in the browser unless they choose to move it.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, approachable, calm, and easy to understand. The interface should feel nice to look at over repeated use, with gentle animation and clear flow rather than decorative flash.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy fintech styling, generic SaaS landing-page visuals, dense spreadsheet clones, and overly technical admin-console aesthetics. The app should not feel noisy, intimidating, salesy, or visually heavy for a salon owner doing practical work.

## Design Principles

- Keep the next action obvious on every screen.
- Make financial state scannable without making the interface feel clinical.
- Prefer approachable workflow guidance over jargon.
- Use motion to clarify change, progress, and completion.
- Treat privacy and local data control as core product trust signals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support reduced motion, keyboard-friendly flows, readable contrast in light and dark themes, and color-safe state communication that does not rely on hue alone.
